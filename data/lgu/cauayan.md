# Cauayan City — LGU Data Pack (Region 2 - Info)

Data compiled September 2026 from LGU/PSA/verification research. Sources: cityofcauayan.gov.ph, PSA 2024 census (PSGC 0203108000), PhilAtlas, verification round 3 (notion doc), 2025 election results.

## identity
- name: Cauayan City
- slug: cauayan
- class: City
- nicknames: "Ideal City of the North", "Rice Bowl of the North", Agro-Industrial Capital of Cagayan Valley, first DOST-designated smart city in PH (2025)
- founded: 1740 (as town of Cagayan province); cityhood: March 30, 2001 (RA 9017, plebiscite)
- etymology: Ilocano "cauayan" = bamboo; town originally sited at Calanusian on the Cagayan River, moved after repeated floods
- district: 3rd legislative district of Isabela (per older data; verify — some sources say 6th under 2025 reapportionment debate)

- income_class: 2nd city income class; revenue ₱1,512M (2024); assets ₱3,634M (2024); poverty incidence 11.71% (2023)

## points (Points tab — unique characteristics)
- First DOST smart city of the Philippines (2025); hosts iSCENE international expo (2023, 2025); 65 barangays with free WiFi (GovNet)
- SM City Cauayan = first SM Supermall in Region 2 — proof of retail gravity
- RC Cola bottling + Ginebra San Miguel plant — agro-industrial anchors
- PRC largest offsite center in PH; SEC regional presence; government-services gravity
- Gawagaway-yan Festival (Gaddang roots, "bountiful livelihood"); Bambanti showcase at SM
- Flood profile: high-risk barangays from Round 2 research: Cabaruan, Alicaocao, Turayong, Baringin Sur, Labinab (100-yr floodplain); SM/Districts I–III core LOW risk; Typhoon Uwan stress test: city on Signal No. 4 (Paolo) historically; 33 region towns dark after Uwan
- CharM EV fast-charging stations live (DOST); e-trikes deployed
- ₱17M DTI Digital Creative Hub; NFA new warehouse groundbreaking
- Le Tour de Filipinas stop-over city
